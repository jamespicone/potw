Bugs!

Character:
- Skitter is determined and driven
- She escalates
- She doesn't back down
- She attacks from unexpected directions
- She keeps trying novel approaches until something works
- She's limited by the bugs she can find
- She multitasks

Themes:
- Resource management
- Weak attacks against lots of targets
- Destroying environment
- Destroying ongoings
- Different damage types?
- Giving players draws?
- Reveals?

Variants:
- Skitter: Base, still learning, aggressive, hurts people, developing tactics?
- Weaver: More experienced, more heroic, refining tactics, doesn't go for the throat quite so hard?
- Khepri: Uses allies powers; hurts herself; sacrifices; doesn't cooperate well.

40 cards, maybe 17 uniques.

Central mechanic of having "strategies" that do things and can have Bug tokens put on them to make them do more things. Bit like omnitron-x but with scaling effects. 

Base power:
- Play a strategy, you may move a bug from a card to another card, or gain a bug.

Incaps:
- Environment deals 1 toxic
- One player draws a card
- Destroy a non character target. Remove skitter from the game.

Strategies:
- x=1+bugs, At end of turn environment deal 1 toxic to X targets (x3)
- when skitter would be damaged, remove 2 bugs from this card, prevent it. (x2)
- x=1+bugs, at end of turn reveal the top X cards of a deck, you may put one in the trash (x2)
- x=1+bugs, at end of turn player draws x (x2)
- at end of turn remove 2 bugs to destroy an ongoing or environment (x2)
- x=1+bugs, at end of turn environment deal x toxic to 1 target (x3)
(14 cards)

Other cards:
- Reshuffle all your bugs however you want ("You may move any number of bug tokens from one of your cards to another one of your cards. You may repeat the full text of this card.") (x3)
- Start of the environment turn gain a bug. Power: reveal until you get a strategy (x2)
- spend x tokens, skitter deals x targets 2 melee and 2 toxic (beeeesss!) (x4)
- 1 hp, reduce melee and projectile dealt by 1, gain 2 bug when enters play, lose 1 bug when dies (x2)
- Increase environment damage to villains by 1. When you deal damage choose damage type. (x2)
- Power: Reveal top 3 cards of a deck; discard all revealed targets; return the remainder in any order. Skitters deals 1 psychic to all villains. (x2)
- Reduce non-fire damage to skitter by 1 (x2)
- Atlas; immune to environment damage, draw a card at end of turn. (x1)
- Spend bugs, destroy x = 1 + bugs ongoings. (x2)
- if your damage is prevented, you may use a power. Power: reveal 3 cards and draw one. (x3)
- Power: Deal 2 irreducible projectile & 1 projectile (x3)
(26 cards)

Planned variants:
	- Skitter: flexible.
	- Weaver: strategies and teamwork; slow and inflexible but benefits the team.
		Gameplay: The first time you play a Strategy card each round, another player may play a card.
			("each round" rather than "each turn" so heroes that make other heroes play cards can't trigger it repeatedly.)
		Power: Either another player draws a card or place a Bug token on up to 2 Strategy cards.
		Incaps:
			- Select a target. Reduce the next damage dealt by that target by 2. (Tying silk to everything while stunned vs. the NY Wards.)
			- One player may play a card. (Contrast with base Skitter's draw.)
			- Reveal the top card of a deck. Discard it or replace it. (Bug surveillance; RevealCard_DiscardItOrPutItOnDeck.)
			No damage incap - deliberate, Weaver doesn't go for the throat. Avoided "one hero uses a power" to keep that Khepri's thing.
	- Taylor Hebert: Cauldron period; Bug cards and bug generation; a little self-destructive.
		Gameplay: Whenever you play a Bug card, draw a card.
		Power: Draw a card. You may discard any number of cards. Place a Bug token on {SkitterCharacter} for each card discarded this way.
		Incaps:
			- The environment deals each target 1 toxic damage. (Self-destructive; hits heroes too. Keep "all targets" - non-hero only would be stronger than any base incap.)
			- Destroy an ongoing card. (Standard; mandatory, so can hit a hero ongoing.)
			- One player may discard a card. If they do, they may draw 2 cards. (The always-good option; echoes the discard-as-fuel power. Cf. Luminary.)
	- Khepri: focuses on her power; rest of the deck exists but isn't driven as much; very self-destructive.
		Gameplay: At the start of your turn, place a Bug token on {SkitterCharacter}.
		Power: Select a hero other than {SkitterCharacter}. That hero uses a power. Then either remove a Bug token from one of your cards or remove one of your non-character cards in play from the game. If you remove a card this way, {SkitterCharacter} deals that hero 2 irreducible psychic damage.
		Incaps:
			- One hero may use a power. (Controlling the heroes.)
			- One player may destroy one of their non-character cards. If they do, they draw a card and prevent the next damage that would be dealt to a hero target. (Sacrifice. The draw refunds the cost - cf. Guise's discard incap. Considered "until start of next turn, prevent all damage to that hero" but rejected: repeatable every turn, so a hero with cheap cards in play could never die to damage. "Non-character" is needed - destroying a hero character card incapacitates it.)
			- Select a non-hero, non-character target. That target deals 1 other target 1 melee damage, then deals itself 1 irreducible psychic damage. (Controlling enemies. Other target first so self-damage can't kill the puppet before it attacks.)
	- Incaps TBD for all variants.
